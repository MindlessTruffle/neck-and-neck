How to play Giraffe Volleyball: Neck & Neck

A free browser volleyball game for desktop and mobile. Play 1v1 or 2v2 online, or pick Bots for a solo match.

Serve and spike

Move sideways along the service line, then click or tap to toss. Click again to serve. During a rally, swing your neck toward the ball and aim across the net. A passive contact sets the ball up. When your head is slightly below an approaching ball, the spike cue tells you when to click for a perfect spike.

Score a point

Land the ball inside the opposing court. Each team gets four touches per possession. A fifth costs a point. In doubles, alternate receivers. Blocks are exempt. Reach seven with a two-point lead, or score eleven to finish.

Play with friends

Choose Party to create a room and share its four-digit code. A party of two can join the 2v2 queue together. Choose Play → Online for matchmaking or Spectate live games to watch a court.

Controls

Move with WASD, jump with Space, set with Q and dive with V. Open quick chat with C, switch groups with the arrow keys, and send with 1–4. On mobile, drag the field to swing and use the on-screen buttons. Turn on advanced moves in the Controls settings.
